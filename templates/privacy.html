1. Who we are

9jaRent.com.ng ("we", "us", "our") is the operator of the 9jaRent property rental marketplace, a service offered to users in Nigeria. For the purposes of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA"), we are the data controller for personal data processed through the Site.

Contact us at support@9jarent.com.ng for any question about this policy or about your data.

2. What data we collect

Data you give us directly:

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed).
  • Agent details (if you register as an agent): agency name, office address, state, city, WhatsApp number, profile photo, description of your agency.
  • Listings: property title, description, address, location, price, fees, amenities, images, video.
  • Messages: the content of conversations you have with other users through the Site.
  • Inspection requests: requested date, time, and the optional note you write to the other party.
  • Reports: the category, description, and target of any report you submit.
  • Preferences: your notification and account settings.

Data we collect automatically:

  • Log data: IP address, timestamps, and the pages or endpoints you access. This is used for security (rate limiting, fraud prevention) and troubleshooting.
  • Session data: a session cookie keeps you logged in between requests.
  • Approximate usage data: aggregate counts (views on a listing, messages sent) that we use to display metrics on your dashboard and to operate the platform.

We do not collect payment card details, bank details, or government identification numbers. If you ever need to send us a government ID for verification purposes, we will ask you to do so directly by email, not through the Site.

3. How we use your data

We use your data to:

  • Create and manage your account, including verifying your email address by sending a one-time code.
  • Display your listings and profile to other users, in line with the visibility settings you choose.
  • Deliver messages, notifications, and inspection requests between you and other users.
  • Moderate the platform — review agent applications, property submissions, and reports.
  • Send you account and system emails (password resets, security alerts, service changes).
  • Send you notification emails about activity on your account, if you have email notifications turned on.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and other security incidents.
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you. Decisions like agent approval or listing removal are made by a human moderator.

4. Legal basis

Under the NDPA, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to give you the account and services you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Site secure, prevent abuse, and improve the service. We balance these against your rights.
  • Consent: for optional things like marketing emails, where we will ask you separately and you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to retain certain records or respond to lawful requests.

You can ask us for more detail on the specific legal basis for any particular processing activity.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Other users, as part of normal use of the Site. When you list a property, your agent name, WhatsApp number, and (optionally) email are shown on the listing. When you send a message, the recipient sees the message and your display name.
  • Service providers who help us run the platform. These include our web host (which stores the database and uploaded images), our email delivery provider (which sends account and notification emails), and — if enabled — a media storage provider. They process data on our instructions only.
  • Authorities, when required by law. For example, in response to a valid court order or a lawful request from a regulator or law enforcement agency.
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of our assets, in which case the successor will be bound by this policy.

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics companies that build cross-site profiles of you.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in and to protect your account from cross-site request forgery. These are essential cookies — the Site cannot work without them, and they are not used for tracking you across other sites.

You can block cookies in your browser settings, but doing so will prevent you from logging in or using most of the Site.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we archive your account rather than immediately erasing it — this is so that the other party's conversation history and inspection records remain intact (see section 8 of the Terms & Conditions). Archived accounts are excluded from normal use of the Site and their listings are removed from public view.

We may keep certain records longer where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to resolve a dispute, prevent fraud, or enforce our Terms. Security logs (IP addresses, timestamps) are kept for a limited period consistent with our security needs.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing — we never store them in plain text.
  • All traffic to the Site is served over HTTPS in production.
  • Access to the database and to administrative tools is restricted.
  • Administrative actions (approving agents, removing listings, resolving reports) are logged so they can be audited.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights or freedoms, we will notify you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as required by the NDPA.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your data, subject to the retention rules described above.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing, in the circumstances the NDPA allows.
  • Portability — receive a copy of the data you have given us in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdraw consent where we relied on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing already done.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise any of these rights, email support@9jarent.com.ng. We will respond within the period required by the NDPA. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on a request.

10. Children

The Site is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11. International transfers

Some of our service providers (for example, email delivery and, if enabled, media storage) may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where we transfer personal data outside Nigeria, we do so only in accordance with the NDPA — typically on the basis of an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, or your explicit consent.

You can ask us for details of the safeguards we rely on for any specific transfer.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top. If the change is material, we will notify you by email or through an in-app notification before it takes effect.

Continued use of the Site after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

For any question about this policy, or to exercise any of your rights, email support@9jarent.com.ng.
